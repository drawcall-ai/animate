animate

Natural human motion for rigged characters. Each game leads its character by physics: on a keyboard WASD moves, Shift runs, Space acts and a drag turns the camera; on a phone a stick on the left moves (run at its rim), the buttons on the right act, and a drag elsewhere turns the camera.

Proof videos of the pull requests

Games

  • BowlingWalk to the ball return, take a ball, and bowl it down the lane.
  • ShooterMove, aim by the mouse or a drag, fire, look down the sights, reload, jump, throw a grenade.
  • BasketballPick the ball up, dribble it as you go, and shoot.
  • FootballRun onto the ball to dribble it, then shoot at the goal.
  • TennisStand to the balls the machine feeds and swing: a forehand or a backhand.
  • VolleyballRun in and spike the ball the setter sets, or pass one tossed back.
  • BoxingMove in the guard and punch the bag; press quickly for jab, cross, hook.
  • GladiatorCut and thrust at the post; hold the shield button to raise the shield.
  • FencingAdvance along the piste and lunge at the target.
  • JavelinFetch a javelin from the rack, run up, and throw it.
  • HurdlesRun at a hurdle and jump to clear it.
  • ShelfTake up a box and put it on the shelf, or down on the floor.
  • ButtonWalk up to a panel and press its button: at the knee, the chest, above the head.
  • PatrolA night guard: raise the torch and search where the camera looks; ◀ ▶ turn.
  • LedWalk, run and jump wherever you like.

Studies

  • RiflemanA soldier led relative to his aim: move, turn, reload, throw a grenade.
  • Gait drillsWalks, runs and turns on a mannequin, with footprints that show a sliding foot.

  • HoldsThe hand holding a sword, a bottle, a phone, a key, a handle, a pen, a pistol.
